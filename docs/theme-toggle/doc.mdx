## Usage

```tsx
import { ThemeToggle } from "@/components/theme-toggle";

<ThemeToggle />
```

A round, glossy button that matches the opposite of your theme: dark with a gold sun in light mode, and light with a dark moon in dark mode. Tap it and the theme flips. The button fades to its new color while the icon turns out and the other one turns in.

The click is soft. The button sinks a little and eases back, and a gentle ripple spreads from where you pressed and fades away. The whole page also fades between light and dark instead of snapping, in browsers that support view transitions (Chrome, Edge and Safari 18+). Set `pageFade={false}` to switch instantly. With a mouse, a spotlight follows the cursor and the rim lights up on the side nearest the pointer.

It also shows the right icon on the very first frame, before the page's JavaScript loads, as long as your theme uses the `dark` class (the next-themes default for shadcn).

It switches your site's theme through [next-themes](https://github.com/pacocoursey/next-themes), the same setup as the shadcn dark mode guide. Wrap your app in its `ThemeProvider` with `attribute="class"` and the toggle works with no props.

Not using next-themes? Pass `theme` and `onThemeChange` and handle the theme yourself:

```tsx
const [theme, setTheme] = useState<"light" | "dark">("light");

<ThemeToggle theme={theme} onThemeChange={setTheme} />
```

Screen readers hear "Switch to dark mode" or "Switch to light mode", and it works with Tab, Enter and Space. With reduced motion on, the icons swap with a fade and there is no squeeze, ripple or page fade.
